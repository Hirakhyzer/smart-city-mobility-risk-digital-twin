# Publication Readiness Plan

This document frames the **Smart City Mobility Risk Digital Twin** as a possible academic research artifact.

## 1. Possible paper framing

A strong paper could frame the repository as:

> A synthetic, auditable digital twin for evaluating congestion, accident risk, emissions burden, emergency response delay, and mobility equity under alternative smart-city policy scenarios.

## 2. Possible research questions

1. How do transparent scenario policies change congestion, safety, emissions, emergency-delay, and equity indicators?
2. Can a synthetic digital twin help stress-test mobility planning workflows before real data integration?
3. Which risk metrics conflict with each other under policy interventions?
4. How can audit trails improve reproducibility and accountability in urban AI planning tools?

## 3. Baseline experiments

Recommended experiments:

- Baseline scenario versus all policy scenarios.
- Congestion-pricing sensitivity analysis.
- Emergency-lane priority under incident-heavy traces.
- Transit-priority corridor under peak demand.
- Low-emission routing under heavy-vehicle traffic.
- Equity-aware mobility under high-burden zone configurations.

## 4. Evidence to include

- Scenario comparison table.
- Congestion and accident-risk figures.
- Emissions-burden figure.
- Emergency-delay audit table.
- Transport-equity audit table.
- Hash-chained audit log summary.
- Clear synthetic-data and non-deployment boundary.

## 5. Review limitations

A publishable version should clearly state that synthetic traces do not validate real city performance. Real-world claims require calibrated data, traffic-engineering validation, emergency-services review, public participation, privacy safeguards, and local governance.

## 6. Future research extensions

- Calibrated traffic traces under approved data governance.
- Multi-agent simulation for road users and transit services.
- Accessibility-aware routing and mobility burden analysis.
- Robustness under extreme-weather and emergency scenarios.
- Privacy-preserving aggregation of mobility traces.
- Human-in-the-loop policy review dashboards.
