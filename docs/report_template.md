# Report Template

## Objective

State the planning question and scenario set.

## Synthetic data scope

Describe the fictional city zones, road network, traffic traces, transit stops, and emergency facilities.

## Scenario comparison

Summarize congestion, accident risk, emissions, emergency delay, equity burden, and composite planning score.

## High-risk segments

List road segments with elevated congestion, accident, or road-vulnerability scores.

## Emergency routing review

Identify zones with elevated estimated response delay and likely drivers.

## Transport equity review

Identify priority zones where mobility burden exceeds the city scenario mean.

## Boundary

Confirm that the result is a synthetic planning review, not official traffic-control, emergency-dispatch, or policy certification guidance.
